2Partial settle — pay some now, the rest stays pending

The ledger already computes pending as a running difference, so a partial transfer needs no new concept — it's just a settle event whose amount is less than the balance. The design keeps one rule visible everywhere: whatever you don't send stays owed, and the gauge doesn't reset. Amount is editable on the settle sheet with a slider plus quick fractions; the confirm state names the leftover explicitly so a short transfer never feels like a bug; History shows partials as a split-fill bar.
